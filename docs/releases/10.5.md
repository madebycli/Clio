## Clio 10.5 (preview)

A preview build for testing. It is not marked as the latest release.

### Fixed

- **Scrolling into the next chapter no longer waits for the details.** Some extensions take minutes to list a long
  chapter list. The next chapter now loads right away, and the "Loading" notice only shows while a chapter loads.
- **Downloaded chapters stay readable with merged chapter groups.** When several groups have the same chapter, the
  downloaded copy is the one listed and opened, with its download mark, instead of a newer upload from another group.
- **Extension updates stay in Explore.** An available update is shown again after the app restarts.

Everything from 10.0 to 10.4 is included.
